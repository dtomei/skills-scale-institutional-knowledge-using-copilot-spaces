# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Works with QA Lead on acceptance testing and quality standards
- Collaborates with Release Manager on deployment preparation
- Supports Risk Owner in identifying technical risks

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Provides guidance to Stakeholder Liaison on requirements and priorities
- Aligns with Communications Lead on messaging around features
- Collaborates with QA Lead on acceptance criteria

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Works closely with Release Manager on delivery schedules
- Receives escalations and updates from Risk Owner
- Coordinates with Communications Lead on stakeholder messaging
- Manages overall timeline with input from all roles

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate all release-related activities, ensuring deployment quality and schedule alignment with stakeholders. They serve as the central point of coordination between development, QA, and operations for all release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules and timelines
- Ensure all deployment prerequisites are met (testing, documentation, approvals)
- Manage release notes, deployment checklists, and rollback procedures
- Coordinate with Developers and QA Lead on release readiness
- Communicate release status and timelines to stakeholders
- Manage post-deployment monitoring and issue tracking

### Goals
- Execute predictable, quality releases on schedule
- Minimize deployment risk and ensure rapid rollback capability
- Maintain clear communication across all release participants
- Ensure compliance with organizational release policies

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and readiness reviews
- Pre-release checklists and deployment coordination
- Post-deployment status reports and incident tracking
- Escalations to Project Manager on release risks or delays

### Key Interactions
- Collaborates with Developers on code deployment and validation
- Works with QA Lead to confirm testing completion and quality gates
- Partners with Project Manager on release scheduling and stakeholder alignment
- Communicates deployment status via Communications Lead
- Escalates deployment risks to Risk Owner for tracking

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads oversee all testing planning, execution, and quality assurance activities. They bridge development and business acceptance by ensuring acceptance criteria are validated and quality standards are maintained throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy, test plans, and acceptance criteria validation
- Coordinate test execution across development and UAT phases
- Identify and track quality issues and defects
- Ensure test coverage aligns with risk profile and acceptance criteria
- Communicate quality status and readiness assessments to Project Manager
- Collaborate on release readiness and deployment validation

### Goals
- Ensure delivered features meet acceptance criteria
- Maintain quality standards and reduce production defects
- Provide clear visibility into test coverage and quality risks
- Enable confident releases through thorough testing

### Typical Communication
- Quality status updates and defect reports
- Test plans and acceptance criteria reviews
- Release readiness assessments
- Collaboration with Developers on test-driven development

### Key Interactions
- Works with Developers on test automation and quality practices
- Validates acceptance criteria with Product Manager
- Provides quality assessments to Release Manager for deployment readiness
- Escalates quality risks to Risk Owner for mitigation planning
- Reports testing status to Communications Lead for stakeholder updates

---

## Communications Lead

### Role Summary
Communications Leads manage internal and external communications for the project, centralizing updates, handling inquiries, and ensuring consistent messaging across all stakeholders. They serve as a hub for information flow and transparency.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain communication plans and stakeholder distribution lists
- Centralize project status updates, decisions, and announcements
- Prepare stakeholder briefings, reports, and executive summaries
- Manage inquiries and feedback from stakeholders
- Ensure consistency in messaging across all communications
- Coordinate with Project Manager and other roles on updates to share

### Goals
- Maintain transparent, consistent communication with all stakeholders
- Ensure timely dissemination of critical project information
- Build stakeholder confidence through clear, regular updates
- Reduce miscommunication and alignment issues

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder updates and status reports
- Executive briefings and milestone announcements
- Centralized decision logs and change notifications
- Inquiry response coordination

### Key Interactions
- Receives status and updates from all roles (Project Manager, Release Manager, QA Lead, Risk Owner)
- Coordinates with Stakeholder Liaison on key stakeholder concerns
- Works with Product Manager on feature and roadmap announcements
- Supports Risk Owner in communicating risk escalations when needed
- Ensures timely communication of release status via Release Manager

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Summary
Risk Owners are assigned responsibility for managing specific project risks. They develop and execute mitigation strategies, track risk status, and escalate issues that may impact project delivery. They work closely with the Project Manager to maintain the risk register and ensure proactive risk management.

### Responsibilities
- Own assigned risks and develop mitigation plans
- Track risk status and mitigation progress
- Identify new risks and escalate emerging concerns
- Collaborate with relevant roles to execute mitigation strategies
- Provide regular risk status updates to Project Manager
- Recommend risk acceptance or escalation decisions

### Goals
- Proactively manage identified risks to prevent issues
- Minimize impact of realized risks on project delivery
- Maintain clear visibility into risk status and mitigations
- Build organizational risk awareness and resilience

### Typical Communication
- Risk status updates and escalations to Project Manager
- Mitigation plan collaboration with relevant team members
- Risk register updates and trend analysis
- Escalation notifications to appropriate stakeholders via Communications Lead

### Key Interactions
- Reports to Project Manager on risk status and mitigation progress
- Collaborates with Developers and QA Lead on technical risks
- Works with Release Manager on deployment risks
- Escalates critical risks via Communications Lead to stakeholders
- Coordinates with other Risk Owners on interconnected risks

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons act as the primary point of contact between the project team and key business stakeholders. They ensure consistent engagement, gather feedback, and maintain strong relationships that support project success and alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Serve as primary contact point for key stakeholders
- Gather and communicate stakeholder feedback and requirements
- Schedule and facilitate stakeholder meetings and reviews
- Ensure stakeholder concerns are documented and addressed
- Maintain stakeholder engagement throughout the project lifecycle
- Provide feedback to Product Manager and Project Manager on stakeholder priorities

### Goals
- Maintain strong stakeholder relationships and engagement
- Ensure stakeholder needs and concerns are understood and addressed
- Provide regular visibility into project progress and decisions
- Build stakeholder confidence and organizational support

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder meetings and one-on-ones
- Feedback collection and escalation
- Attendance at key project milestones and reviews
- Coordination with Communications Lead on updates

### Key Interactions
- Receives product and roadmap guidance from Product Manager
- Works with Project Manager on overall timeline and delivery status
- Escalates stakeholder concerns to Communications Lead for broader communication
- Gathers feedback to inform priority and decision-making
- Supports Release Manager in managing stakeholder expectations around deployments
